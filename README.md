This application converts any amount in Rands (ZAR) to currencies around the world and is useful for travel and business. I was personally motivated to create an app like this after having travelled to a different country and realizing we had completely overlooked exchange rates and had no idea how much our money was really worth in this foreign country. The focus is usually on the dollar, euro and pound that it is that much harder getting consistent, reliable information about currency conversions from one's local currency to the currencies of other countries that aren't considered market superpopwers. I have included the currency conversions for the countries most South Africans find themselves either visiting or trading with. In the way of future plans, I will be incorporating an API so the information stays updated and accurate.
